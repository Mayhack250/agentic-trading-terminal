## 2. Interactive Research Copilot Chat (Researchers Only)
Interact directly with the research team via a dedicated natural language terminal:
- **Direct Scope Filtering**: Direct your query to `[ALL RESEARCHERS]`, `[BULL ONLY]`, `[BEAR ONLY]`, `[VALUATION ONLY]`, or `[TECHNICAL ONLY]`.
- **Frictionless Quick Prompts**: One-click research triggers:
  - `[BULL THESIS]`: Returns upside target, core catalyst, and valuation coverage.
  - `[BEAR RISKS]`: Returns downside invalidation stop, multiple compression risks, and concentration flags.
  - `[DCF FAIR VALUE]`: Computes forward P/E, DCF spread, and cash flow yield.
  - `[SEC & CATALYSTS]`: Audits regulatory dockets, headline sentiment, and options put/call volume.
  - `[EXECUTIVE SUMMARY]`: Synthesizes the overall conviction score and recommended execution levels.
- **Freeform Natural Language**: Type any question (e.g. *"Why is the Bear cautious on TSLA?"* or *"What is the fair value according to DCF?"*) and receive immediate quantitative answers grounded in current asset state.

## 3. Real-Time Broker Execution Desk & Demo Account
- **Account Mode Switcher**:
  - `DEMO / PAPER ($100K)`: Full paper trading simulation with live balance tracking ($100,000 starting cash, equity curve, margin, realized and unrealized PnL).
  - `LIVE BROKER`: Connect to Alpaca Markets, Interactive Brokers (TWS / IB Gateway port 7497), or Binance/Coinbase via the Broker Config Modal.
- **Order Types**: `LIMIT`, `MARKET`, `STOP LIMIT`, and `BRACKET (TP/SL)`.
- **Accurate Position Sizing**:
  - Equity Risk presets: `[1% RISK]`, `[2% RISK]`, and `[5% MAX]`. Automatically computes the exact share quantity according to the distance between current price and the Risk Sentinel's stop loss.
  - Live calculation of total dollar commitment and % of account equity.
- **Live Open Positions Ledger**:
  - Tracks open holdings in real time.
  - Every market tick updates the unrealized PnL ($ and %) of all active positions.
  - `[CLOSE]`: Liquidates position at current market price and credits cash.
  - `[TRIM 50%]`: Partially sells half the position to lock in gains.
- **Reset Demo Account**: One-click reset restores the paper account to $100,000.00 cash.

## 4. Synchronized Market Engine
No lagging panels—the entire console moves together:
- When you switch tickers (NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, AMD, SPY, BTC/USD, ETH/USD, or Custom Symbol):
  - Candlestick chart re-renders historical bars and indicators.
  - Researcher targets, stops, forward P/E, RSI, and conviction bars update instantly.
  - Order ticket auto-syncs prices (Limit, Stop Loss, Take Profit) and recalculates share sizing.
  - Research chat outputs an asset briefing.
- When the market ticks (every 1s, 3s, or 5s):
  - Last quote, delta, spread, VWAP, high, and low flash with micro-pulses.
  - Chart candle updates high, low, close, and volume.
  - Open positions recalculate unrealized profit/loss.
  - Account total equity recalculates.

## 5. ADHD Cognitive Architecture & Sensory Ergonomics
Designed specifically to eliminate cognitive fatigue, time blindness, and decision paralysis:
1. **Focus Mode (`F` or `[FOCUS: OFF/ON]`)**:
   - Dims peripheral panels and spotlights the chart, current quote, and broker execution ticket. Eliminates visual overwhelm when preparing an order.
2. **Command Palette (`Ctrl+K` or `Cmd+K`)**:
   - Frictionless keyboard navigation to jump between tickers, execute trades, trigger research, or toggle settings without mouse fatigue.
3. **Pre-Flight Invalidation Safeguard Checklist**:

4. **Tactile Acoustic Synthesizer (Pure Web Audio)**:
   - Ultra-responsive mechanical clicks, soft tick hums, alert pings for shock injections, and an uplifting C-Major chord chime when orders fill.

   - Live session stopwatch in the footer (`00:24:18`) to combat ADHD time blindness during trading hours.
6. **Information Chunking**:
   - High-contrast, scannable data chips; zero cognitive strain from walls of text.
